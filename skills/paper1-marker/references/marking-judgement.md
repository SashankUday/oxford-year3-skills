# Interpreting the descriptors

This is derived guidance. The original table in official-criteria.md governs assessment.

## Band distinctions

| Listed marks | Overall descriptor and important distinction |
| --- | --- |
| 90, 93, 96, 99, 100 | Truly outstanding: extensive range/depth and evidence, high degree of original critical assessment and independent opinion grounded in experimental methods/evidence. |
| 81, 84, 87 | Outstanding: high degree of range/depth, varied evidence and moderate degree of original critical assessment and independent opinion. |
| 72, 75, 78 | Excellent: relevant range/depth, varied evidence and some original critical assessment and independent opinion. |
| 66, 69 | Very good: question-focused understanding and application, relevant information and clear arguments supported by experimental evidence. |
| 60, 63 | Good: similar foundation, potentially slightly deficient relevant breadth or experimental evidence. |
| 51, 54, 57 | Major points and some understanding; some misconceptions may be present. |
| 42, 45, 48 | Poor construction and insufficient relevant detail; some knowledge but misconceptions. |
| 30, 33, 36, 39 | Minimal, short/insubstantial attempt with some relevant accurate material. |
| 3 through 27, in steps of 3 | Minimal, short/insubstantial attempt with very little relevant material. |
| 0 | No answer, or wholly irrelevant or inaccurate. |

Read the full descriptors before applying them. Do not assign marks by counting criticisms or papers. Do not average a set of invented component scores.

## Avoid false precision and false ceilings

The earlier AI marking guide proposed rigid ceilings for weak focus and any genuine misconception. Those exact automatic caps are not stated in the official table and are not adopted here. The absence of meaningful evaluation makes a first-class descriptor difficult to justify, but assess the whole answer rather than executing a keyword rule. Consider whether an error is central, repeated and damaging, or a local slip in an otherwise strong argument.

An explicit thesis sentence is helpful but not mandatory. A sustained argument can be identifiable without a formulaic introduction. A bibliography, recent citation, diagram, or particular paragraph count is not itself a first-class discriminator. Judge the job each performs.

## Range with depth

“Depth over breadth” in two tutor comments means trimming peripheral review material to analyse the selected evidence. It does not mean making every question artificially narrow. Cover every part of the question with sufficient relevant range, then develop mechanisms and inference. A question about cAMP in depression differs from one about cAMP across disease causation and treatment.

## Criticism with consequences

Use the chain: feature of the design → plausible interpretative consequence → narrower conclusion or useful discriminating test.

“Only males were studied” is not enough on its own. Explain whether a relevant sex-dependent mechanism or target population makes generalisation uncertain; do not invent such a mechanism. “Ex vivo” can be important if cutting tissue disrupts the connectivity the experiment claims to measure. It is not a generic reason to dismiss all slice experiments.

Critique strengths as well as weaknesses: a rescue, specificity control, complementary measurement or convergence across models can resolve a particular alternative. Observational and descriptive work may be the appropriate evidence for parts of the question; do not universally treat it as inferior or unusable.

## Factual status

Separate: error supported by a supplied primary source; tutor correction; concern needing verification; and a limitation of what the available evidence proves. Absence of a study from the essay does not establish that it does not exist. No source access means no claimed fact-check.

## Clinical implications

The iron-deficiency tutor requested a stronger bridge from mechanism to manipulation of pulmonary pathophysiology. Build this bridge when relevant to the question and thesis. Do not force a clinical-treatment section into every basic-science essay, or append a memorised heart-failure plan to a pulmonary-specific question. Keep proposed mechanisms distinct from demonstrated patient benefit.

## Format and timing

The allostery essay was judged too long and too much like a review/book chapter. The tutor did not specify a universal word limit. For timed practice, judge selectivity and realistic use of the allotted time. For tutorial work, respect tutor-specified formatting and bibliography expectations; do not invent timed-exam penalties.
