# Historical Paper 1 assessment criteria: 2025

This is a paraphrase of the table headed “Criteria for assessing individual questions within Papers 1 and 2”, Annex A, page 90 of the **FHS Medical Sciences, CSB Part B and Neurosciences Part B Examiners' Report 2025**. The original report was inspected when building the skill. It is retained in the creator's local source collection and is not redistributed here. Read any applicable current convention supplied by the student before marking.

| Listed marks | Descriptor, paraphrased |
| --- | --- |
| 90, 93, 96, 99, 100 | Truly outstanding response to the set question; clear logical structure; extensive range and depth; extensive evidence from diverse sources; highly original critical assessment and independent judgement grounded in experimental evidence and methods. |
| 81, 84, 87 | Outstanding question-focused response; strong range and depth; varied sources; a moderate degree of original critical assessment and independent judgement founded on evidence and methods. |
| 72, 75, 78 | Excellent response; clear structure, relevant range/depth, varied evidence and some original critical assessment and independent judgement. |
| 66, 69 | Very good, focused answer showing understanding and application, relevant information and clear experimentally supported arguments. |
| 60, 63 | Good, focused answer showing understanding/application and supported argument, potentially slightly deficient in relevant breadth or experimental evidence. |
| 51, 54, 57 | Major points covered with some understanding; misconceptions may be present. |
| 42, 45, 48 | Poorly constructed and lacking relevant detail; some knowledge but misconceptions. |
| 30, 33, 36, 39 | Minimal, short or insubstantial response containing some relevant accurate material. |
| 3–27 in steps of 3 | Minimal, short or insubstantial response containing very little relevant material. |
| 0 | No answer, or an answer wholly irrelevant or inaccurate. |

These descriptors assess the overall response. They do not specify a numerical weighting for dimensions, a paper-count quota, or a universal word limit. The table's discrete marks should not overwrite a recorded tutorial grade such as 68%.

## Examiner commentary, paraphrased

Page 14, Option A: stronger essays critically used relevant evidence and answered the exact wording; weaker essays supplied general information poorly tailored to it, sometimes misunderstanding cited evidence. The broad cAMP question required consideration of disease causation and treatment across diseases, so a depression-only answer could be insufficient for that question, even if suitable for a depression-specific tutorial.

Page 16, Option B: mechanisms, experimental support, structure and evaluation distinguished stronger essays from superficial descriptions.

Page 82: the historical Medicine Paper 1 format was a three-hour paper with three answers on different themes from at least two options. This is whole-paper context, not a penalty for an isolated practice essay. Use current instructions for current requirements.
