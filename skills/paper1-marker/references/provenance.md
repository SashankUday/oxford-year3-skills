# Sources and publication scope

Built from Sashank's Year 3 resources and feedback on four tutorial essays, 7 October 2026.

The 2025 official report's criteria table (Annex A, page 90), Paper 1 commentary (pages 14 and 16), and paper-format statement (page 82) informed the historical criteria summary. The public edition paraphrases the criteria and records their provenance. Current applicable original criteria supplied by a student take precedence.

Four annotated essays informed tutor-feedback.md: pituitary networks (81%); iron deficiency and hypoxia (68%); cAMP and depression (qualitative comments only); GPCR allostery (qualitative comments only). Explicit PDF comments were extracted and rendered pages reviewed. The public annotation record retains 36 comments and page provenance while omitting personal author metadata and identifying filenames. No grade was invented where none was recorded.

The original PDFs, full course-source collection and source-backed skill package remain in the creator's local collection. They are not needed for routine use but are required if a disputed detail cannot be established from the summaries. This public package does not provide original essays for independent calibration.

Prior derived guides and Sashank's later clarifications informed the operational workflow. Unsupported automatic caps, fixed paragraph/word counts and universal formatting rules from older AI guides were not carried forward. Personal preparation estimates remain advice rather than marking criteria.

The two recorded grades are examples, not evidence of independent predictive accuracy. Structural validation and installation tests do not validate grading accuracy. Future calibration should use unseen essays with tutor marks/comments hidden from the marking model.
